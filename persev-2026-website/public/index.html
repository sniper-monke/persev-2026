01The Story

Five years of transformative culture.

Perseverantia 2026 celebrates the spirit of resilience, evolution, and shared ambition. Each year, Perseverantia draws students from schools across the city to Bombay Scottish School, Mahim, where they come together to share ideas, perform and test their skills across a wide spectrum of events. Grounded in excellence and integrity, the fest is a space where ambition meets purpose and creativity meets community.

The theme for Perseverantia's 5th edition, 'Metamorphosis', invites participants to embrace transformation and the courage to grow. Much like a butterfly emerging from its chrysalis, the theme reflects how we evolve with changing times, learning to shed the masks society asks us to wear and reinvent ourselves in our truest form.

Every event has been thoughtfully crafted to embrace the opportunity to evolve, making this edition of Perseverantia not just a celebration of talent, but of growth, identity and the strength to become who we truly are.
